Build with AI · IPN AI Youth Summit

Build your own AI assistant.

In one hour, you'll set up an AI assistant on your own laptop that can chat, read your files, and talk out loud. Then you'll make it yours: a research partner for class, a job-hunt sidekick, or the engine behind a side hustle.

No experience needed. This session is listed as Intermediate / Advanced, but if you can copy and paste, you can do this. We'll guide you through every step.
