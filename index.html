Get set up · at your own pace

Five small steps. That's it.

Pick your computer, then open one step at a time. Most people finish in 30–45 minutes — and nothing here can break your laptop.

It's normal if some of this is new. Go at your own speed, check things off as you go, and if a step won't budge, leave it — we'll sort it out together in the room.

In the room · Sept 12 · 3–4 PM

From starter bot to something that's yours.

Your challenge: a useful opportunity briefing for a field you care about. We'll build a small working version first, together.

The finish line

A briefing you'd actually come back to.

Choose a field. Bring a few articles, job posts, or company notes. Help your bot turn that into a clear next step.

  1. 01 Companies & industry signals
  2. 02 Roles, internships & events
  3. 03 People to learn from
  4. 04 One action to take next

Start with real material you bring. Hermes begins as a chat bot, not a web researcher. Paste in your own text first. Live search and scheduled briefings are stretch goals, not part of the starter.

Step 01 · Understand

Get your bearings.

Open your hermes-bot folder in OpenChamber and ask it to explain the project before changing anything.

Paste in your coding assistant 
Read this project and explain how a message travels from the browser to OpenAI and back. Point out where the bot's instructions live. Don't change any files yet.
Step 02 · Build

Make one useful change.

Add inputs for your field and source material. Keep this first version small enough to test quickly.

Paste in your coding assistant 
Turn Hermes into a personal opportunity briefing tool.
Add inputs for my field, goal, and pasted source material.
Produce a brief with companies, opportunities, people, and one next action.
Use only the material I provide. Link sources only when I include their URLs. Say when information is missing; don't invent jobs, people, dates, or citations.
Keep the API key on the server. Propose a small plan first, then help me implement and test it.
Step 03 · Test & share

Make it work. Then make it better.

Restart the server after backend changes, refresh the browser, and try a real example.

Feeling good? Try one stretch goal — conversation memory, saving a brief, or live retrieval with sources. Pick one, not all three.
